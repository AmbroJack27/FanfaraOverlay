## 🇬🇧 ENGLISH

### Title (pick one)
- Fanfara: epic Steam achievement notifications — free & open source

### Description
Fanfara turns Steam's forgettable achievement popup into a celebratory, animated notification — with a different orchestral sound depending on how rare the achievement is (from Common to Legendary).

✨ What it does
• Automatically detects the achievement's rarity
• Animated notifications with many styles to choose from
• Dedicated orchestral sounds for each rarity
• Works in-game, even with Frame Generation and exclusive fullscreen
• Runs quietly in the Windows tray
• Free, no ads, open source
