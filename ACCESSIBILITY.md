# Accessibility

> 🇮🇹 Questo progetto tiene all'accessibilità. Puoi segnalare un problema di accessibilità
> **in italiano o in inglese** aprendo una [issue](../../issues).

Fanfara is a visual overlay that makes Steam achievement unlocks easier to notice and enjoy. We want
it to be usable by as many people as possible, and we welcome feedback on how to do better.

## What Fanfara does for accessibility

- **Adjustable duration** — notifications can stay on screen longer (up to 12 seconds), so there is
  time to read them.
- **Large, high-contrast notifications** — designed to be readable on big and high-resolution
  monitors.
- **Light and dark themes** for the settings window.
- **Sound cues** — every style has a built-in sound, so an unlock is noticeable even without looking
  at the screen; the rarer the achievement, the more prominent the sound.
- **Rarity shown in more than one way** — rarity is conveyed by text (the tier name and percentage),
  an icon, *and* color together, not by color alone.
- **Choice of on-screen position** — 7 positions, so notifications can be placed where they are
  easiest for you to see.
- **Five languages** — English, Italian, French, German and Spanish, auto-detected from your Windows
  language.
- **No interaction required** — notifications are informational and click-through; they never steal
  focus or block input in the game.

## Supported environment

- Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). The app is a lightweight native overlay and does not require special
  hardware.

## Known limitations

- Fanfara is a **visual + audio** notification; there is currently no screen-reader announcement of
  unlocks.
- Sounds are synthesized and, for now, cannot be individually replaced with your own audio.
- The on-screen box appears over games in **borderless window** mode; from v0.1.10 Fanfara prepares
  your games automatically so it also shows over fullscreen titles.

## Reporting an accessibility barrier

If something is hard to see, hear, read or use, please tell us — it helps everyone:

- Open an [issue](../../issues) (the *Bug report* or *Feature request* template is fine), **or**
- For anything sensitive, use the private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Please include what was difficult, what would help, and your setup (Windows version, monitor,
assistive tools you use). We read every report. 🙏
