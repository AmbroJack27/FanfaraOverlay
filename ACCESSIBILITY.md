# Accessibility

Fanfara makes Steam achievement unlocks easier to notice and enjoy. We want it to be usable by as
many people as possible, and we welcome feedback on how to do better.

## What Fanfara does for accessibility

**Notifications**
- **Adjustable duration** — up to 12 seconds on screen, so there is time to read them.
- **Large, high-contrast notifications**, drawn sharp on big and high-resolution monitors.
- **Sound cues** — every style has its own sound, so an unlock is noticeable without looking at the
  screen; the rarer the achievement, the more prominent the sound. The volume is adjustable.
- **Rarity shown in more than one way** — the tier name and percentage (text), an emblem *and* a
  color, never color alone.
- **7 on-screen positions** and a choice of **monitor**, so notifications appear where you can see
  them best.
- **No interaction required** — notifications are click-through: they never steal focus or block
  input in the game.

**The app**
- **Light and dark theme** on every page (Home, Customize, History, Collection, Leaderboard, Guide).
- **Five languages** — English, Italian, French, German and Spanish — following Windows or your
  choice, including the installer.
- A **guided setup** walks through every option step by step, and the in-app **Guide** explains each
  feature in plain words.
- Clear, worded notices (for example the red Steam-privacy notice says exactly what to change and
  links to the right page).
- On collection cards, owned / missing states use a **text label and an icon**, not color alone.

## Supported environment

- Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). No special hardware needed.

## Known limitations

- Unlocks are announced **visually and with sound**; there is no screen-reader announcement yet.
- The app's pages are built for mouse use first; keyboard navigation works in most places but is not
  complete.
- The app follows its own size (browser zoom with Ctrl +/− is turned off to keep things sharp); use
  Windows display scaling to make everything larger.
- Sounds are part of each style and cannot yet be replaced with your own audio.
- In games running in **exclusive fullscreen**, the box may not be visible (the sound still plays):
  Fanfara prepares games automatically, and the guided setup explains how to pick borderless or
  non-exclusive fullscreen.

## Reporting an accessibility barrier

If something is hard to see, hear, read or use, please tell us — it helps everyone:

- Open an [issue](../../issues) (the *Bug report* or *Feature request* template is fine),
- use **Send feedback** inside the app, **or**
- for anything sensitive, the private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Please include what was difficult, what would help, and your setup (Windows version, monitor,
assistive tools you use). We read every report. 🙏

## 🇮🇹 In breve (italiano)

- **Notifiche**: durata fino a 12 secondi, grandi e ad alto contrasto, un suono per ogni stile
  (più è raro, più si sente), rarità indicata con testo, emblema e colore insieme, 7 posizioni e scelta
  dello schermo, mai invadenti (non rubano il focus al gioco).
- **App**: tema chiaro e scuro su ogni pagina, 5 lingue (anche l'installer), una configurazione guidata
  passo passo e una Guida con parole semplici; sulle carte «Ce l'hai / Ti manca» c'è sempre testo e
  icona, non solo il colore.
- **Limiti**: non c'è ancora l'annuncio per lettori di schermo; la tastiera funziona quasi ovunque ma
  non del tutto; lo zoom Ctrl +/− è disattivato (usa il ridimensionamento di Windows); nei giochi a
  schermo intero esclusivo il riquadro può non vedersi (il suono sì).
- **Segnalazioni**: apri una [issue](../../issues), usa **Invia feedback** nell'app oppure, per cose
  delicate, il canale privato di [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
